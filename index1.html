Our Story

Built on Point-of-Laying Birds, Growing Into a Full Farm Supply Business

Aishdot Poultry Farm grew out of a commitment to put fresh, reliable poultry products within reach of families and businesses in Lafia. What began as a project around point-of-laying birds has grown into a full poultry and agricultural supply business — one that creates local jobs, supports other agricultural businesses through the feed and supplies we buy, and keeps food and money moving within our community.
