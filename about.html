About Us

About Aishdot Poultry Farm

Aishdot Poultry Farm is a poultry and agricultural services business based in Lafia, Nasarawa State. Our main focus is commercial egg production, built around point-of-laying birds, alongside the supply of live chicken, frozen chicken, feed and supplements to households and businesses across the area.

AISHDOT Poultry Farm
Our Mission

To give our customers fresh, quality and reliable poultry products — while supporting local food supply and agricultural activity in Lafia and its surrounding communities.

Our Process

From Point-of-Laying Birds to Your Table

01

Point-of-Laying Birds

We start with quality point-of-laying birds — the foundation of a reliable egg enterprise.

02

Feeding & Management

Proper feeding and day-to-day management keep our birds healthy and productive.

03

Biosecurity & Health

Strict biosecurity practices protect our flock and the quality of every egg produced.

04

Egg Production

Well-managed birds lay consistently, giving us a steady, dependable supply.

05

Collection, Sorting & Packaging

Eggs are collected, sorted and packaged carefully so freshness is never compromised.

06

Delivery & Sale

Fresh products reach individual customers, retailers, food vendors and restaurants across Lafia.

Products & Services

What Aishdot Poultry Farm Offers

Alongside our core egg enterprise, we supply:

  • Fresh eggs, sold by the crate or in smaller quantities
  • Live chicken, raised under proper feeding and management
  • Frozen chicken, processed and stored for convenience
  • Chicken feeds for households and other poultry keepers
  • Chicken supplements that support bird health
  • Poultry and agricultural services and guidance
